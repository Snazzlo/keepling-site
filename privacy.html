Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

Keepling does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • Your focus sessions are stored on your device and in your own private iCloud account, so they follow you to your other iPhones. Only you can see them; we have no access to your iCloud data.
  • Settings stay on your device.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • The app connects only to Apple: iCloud for syncing your sessions, and the App Store for purchases.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device, only to tell you when a session ends or when your keepling needs you.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device. Your iCloud copy can be removed in Settings › your name › iCloud.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the app. Questions? See the support page.
