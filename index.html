Support

Grow plants, pets and critters while you focus. Pick a keepling, start the timer, and keep your phone down while it grows.

Why did my keepling wilt or get sad?

In Deep Focus mode, leaving Keepling for more than 30 seconds wilts it. Locking your phone is always fine. Prefer to pause? Switch to Gentle mode in Settings.

How do I unlock new keeplings?

Free keeplings unlock as you focus, from 1 hour up to 20 hours of total focus. Tap any keepling in the Collection tab to see how close you are.

How do rare keeplings work?

Each rare keepling is a one-time purchase that's yours forever. Tap it in the Collection tab, or open Settings → Shop rare keeplings. Purchases come back automatically on any iPhone signed into the same Apple Account; you can also tap Restore Purchases.

Does my history move to a new iPhone?

Yes. Your focus sessions sync privately through your iCloud account. Make sure you're signed into iCloud on both phones.

I don't get notifications

Open the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → Keepling, and turn on Allow Notifications.
